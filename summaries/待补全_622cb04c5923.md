## 一句话概括

Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) have achieved no table advancements across various domains. Yet, they remain vulnerable to Side-Channel Attacks (SCAs) which expose them to model theft, posing substantial security threats. Existing countermeasures for mitigating SCAs using DNN obfuscation often involve impractical hardware modifications or suffer from poor scalability due to the need for time-consuming deployment on target platforms. We introduce CLOb-DNN (Contrastive Learning Obfuscation for DNNs), a novel contrastive learning based framework that improves both the efficiency and robust ness of DNN obfuscation against SCAs. By leveraging triplet networks, we effectively reduce the need for repeated physical deployments, substantially improving obfuscation efficiency while ensuring that the obfuscated DNNs meet latency constraints. Our framework integrates architecture-level and scheduling-level obfuscation to enhance stability and avoid costly modifications to backend source codes. We perform extensive evaluations using multiple state-of-the-art attack models to demonstrate the robustness of our approach against diverse threats. Experiments show that our method reduces obfuscation time by approximately 90%, achieves superior defense capability, and successfully meets various latency requirements, thereby offering a practical and effective solution for protecting DNN IPs against SCAs.
