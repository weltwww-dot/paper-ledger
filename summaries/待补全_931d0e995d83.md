## 一句话概括

Atrial fibrillation (AF) is the most common sustained cardiac arrhythmia, affecting nearly 59 million individuals worldwide by 2019 and significantly increasing stroke risk. This work presents a novel hybrid Complex Convolutional Network for automated AF detection using Gramian Angular Field (GAF) transformations of electrocardiogram signals. The proposed method converts one-dimensional ECG segments into two-dimensional image representations through Gramian Angular Summation Field (GASF) and Gramian Angular Difference Field (GADF) transformations, preserving temporal dependencies as geometric relationships in polar coordinates. Our proposed model treats GASF and GADF as real and imaginary components of complex-valued inputs, enabling phase-aware feature extraction through the proposed hybrid architecture combining realvalued and complex-valued convolutional layers. The network incorporates complex convolutions, modified ReLU activations, and residual connections to capture subtle magnitude-phase interactions critical for detecting AF episodes. Evaluated on the PhysioNet AF dataset containing 1,436 ECG recordings, this model achieves superior performance with 95.63% test accuracy, 96.43% precision, 92.05% recall, and 94.15% F1-score, outperforming state-of-the-art models by +1.52% accuracy and +2.1% F1-score. Interpretability analysis using Grad-CAM and Integrated Gradients reveals class-specific attention patterns, with the model focusing on GADF for normal rhythm detection and GASF for AF classification, demonstrating clinically relevant pattern recognition capabilities of our model.
