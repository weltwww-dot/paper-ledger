## 一句话概括

Deploying Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) swarm for Forest Fire Detection (FFD) in hostile environments poses critical dependability challenges to decentralized learning systems, where UAVs are vulnerable to damage from extreme heat, turbulent air currents, and dense smoke. Such damage can lead to data loss and compromise the effectiveness of FFD missions. To address these challenges, we propose a Robust Gossip Learning Framework (RGLF), a fault-tolerant and decentralized learning framework that enhances the robustness and adaptability of UAV swarm-based FFD missions. RGLF introduces a gravitational clustering method to organize UAVs into dispersed clusters, a gossip backup method for decentralized data redundancy, and an adaptive gossipmonger selection method that dynamically designates representative UAVs for model training, preventing mission failures due to the gossipmonger incapacitation. By eliminating reliance on a central coordinator, RGLF enables fault-tolerant model updating through decentralized gossip learning, ensuring continuous learning under the UAV incapacitation. Experimental results demonstrate that RGLF can significantly enhance the mission performance, even when some UAVs are incapacitated. By strengthening the UAV swarm robustness, RGLF provides a more dependable and scalable solution for UAV swarm-based FFD missions.
