## 一句话概括

The Colored Traveling Salesman Problem (CTSP) and its extended variant, the Cumulative Capacitated Colored TSP (C2-CTSP), represent significant generalizations of the classical Multiple TSP (MTSP). By incorporating color constraints, capacity restrictions, and customized cost calculation mechanisms for different entities, these formulations demonstrate strong relevance to real-world applications. This paper proposes an Adaptive Enhanced Attention (AEA) model to address color-constrained traveling salesman problems based on a reinforcement learning framework. Our method introduces several novel techniques. First, we develop a dynamic color-aware decoding mechanism that systematically handles color constraints through bitwise operations and adaptive state transitions. Then, we establish a bi-objective optimization method integrating an adaptive penalty mechanism to balance cumulative cost minimization with constraint satisfaction. In addition, we design an Enhanced Attention Unit (EAU) that accelerates strategy convergence and improves solution precision through gated activation and hierarchical feature propagation. Evaluations on CTSP and C2-CTSP benchmarks demonstrate that the AEA model outperforms existing neural combinatorial optimization (NCO) solvers and several heuristic baselines. Generalization experiments reveal that the EAU module effectively accelerates convergence and enhances decoding robustness, showing promising applicability to other combinatorial optimization tasks.
